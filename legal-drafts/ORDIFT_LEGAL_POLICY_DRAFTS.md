# Ordift Studios — Legal Policy Drafts

**STATUS: FIRST DRAFT — NOT LEGAL ADVICE — NOT YET APPROVED — NOT YET PUBLISHED**

These nine documents are first-draft legal policies prepared for Ordift
Studios ahead of public launch. They are written specifically for Ordift
Studios's business model (a multidisciplinary creative house offering
photography, videography, design, branding, content creation, and talent
services, operating primarily in Ghana with room to expand
internationally) rather than copied from another company's policies.

**They are not legal advice and must not be published or relied upon
until:**

1. **Ordift Studios's owner(s) confirm every bracketed `[business
   decision]`** below — deposit percentages, cancellation windows, fee
   amounts, turnaround times, and similar figures are informed
   first-draft proposals, not settled facts. Nothing in this document
   should be read as an existing Ordift Studios policy until confirmed.
2. **A Ghana-qualified lawyer reviews the full set**, in particular the
   references to the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), the Copyright
   Act, 2005 (Act 690), the Electronic Transactions Act, 2008 (Act 772),
   and the Contracts Act, 1960 (Act 25) — statutory references below are
   believed accurate but must be verified and are not a substitute for
   professional legal review, especially given how frequently regulatory
   detail changes.
3. Only after both of the above should any page be marked `isApproved`
   in the CMS or `LEGAL_PAGES_APPROVED` be set to `true` in production.

We process personal data on the following bases: **performance of a
contract** (delivering a booked service or workshop you registered for);
**consent** (marketing communications, non-essential cookies); **legal
obligation** (tax and financial record-keeping); and **legitimate
interest** (operating and securing the platform, preventing fraud,
responding to enquiries before any contract exists), balanced against
your rights and interests.

### 1.5 Who we share data with

We share personal data only where necessary, with: staff and
contractors who need it to deliver your project (bound by
confidentiality); our email delivery provider (Resend), for

for public-facing content only — client personal data is never stored
in the public content system; and any professional advisor, auditor, or
authority where required by law. **We do not sell personal data**, and
we do not share client personal data with third-party vendors, models,
or instructors beyond what is strictly necessary to deliver a booked
collaborative project, and only with your knowledge.

|---|---|

### 4.3 Cancellation by the client

| Notice before session | Outcome |
|---|---|

how disputes are resolved, and should be read with our
[Booking Policy](#3-booking-policy) and [Cancellation & Rescheduling

Because Ordift Studios reserves a specific date, allocates staff and
equipment, and often begins preparation as soon as a booking is
confirmed, **deposits are non-refundable** except as set out below.

accordance with the agreed scope are not refundable.

A refund (in whole or in part) is available where: the booking is
cancelled within `[7]` days of being made and before any work has begun

reasons within its control (see [Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy
§4.5](#4-cancellation--rescheduling-policy)); a Force Majeure Event
prevents delivery and rescheduling is not possible (see [Terms &
Conditions §2.10](#2-terms--conditions)); or Ordift Studios determines,
after investigating a quality complaint under §5.4, that delivered work
materially failed to meet the agreed scope and cannot reasonably be
corrected.

### 5.3 How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are returned using the original payment method where
possible, within `[e.g. 14 business days]` of approval. Ordift Studios
is not responsible for delays caused by the client's bank or mobile-
money provider.

### 5.4 Quality concerns and complaints

If a client is unsatisfied with delivered work, they should raise the
specific concern in writing within `[e.g. 14 days]` of delivery. Ordift
Studios will first offer reasonable revisions consistent with the
originally agreed scope (see [Client Deliverables
Policy](#8-client-deliverables-policy)) before any refund is considered.
A change of mind about a style, concept, or creative direction that was
approved by the client at an earlier stage (e.g. a signed mood board,
brief, or concept sign-off) is not, by itself, grounds for a refund.

### 5.5 Chargebacks

See [Terms & Conditions §2.8](#2-terms--conditions). Clients are asked
to use this Refund Policy's resolution process before initiating a

consequences described in §2.8.

**Last updated: `[date of approval]`**

This Policy explains who owns the copyright in work created by Ordift
Studios, what clients are permitted to do with delivered work, and how
Ordift Studios protects its own and its clients' intellectual property.
It is grounded in Ghana's **Copyright Act, 2005 (Act 690)**, under which
copyright in a photograph, film, or design vests in its creator (or the
creator's employer/commissioning arrangement, where applicable) unless
expressly assigned in writing.

### 7.1 Ownership

Unless a separate written agreement expressly assigns copyright to the
client, **Ordift Studios retains full copyright ownership** of all
photographs, video footage, designs, branding assets, and other creative
work it produces, including unused frames, outtakes, and RAW/source
files. Booking a session or project licenses the client to use the
delivered final work as set out in §7.2 — it does not transfer
ownership.

### 7.2 Licence granted to clients

On full payment, Ordift Studios grants the client a **non-exclusive,
non-transferable, worldwide licence** to use the final delivered work
for the client's own personal or business purposes (for example,
website use, social media, print marketing, portfolios, or internal
business use), unless the signed quote specifies a different or broader
commercial licence. This standard licence does **not** include the right
to: sell, sub-license, or redistribute the work to third parties;
use the work in a way that implies endorsement by Ordift Studios or its
team without permission; remove or obscure any watermark or credit where
one has been applied; or use the work in advertising for a different
business/brand than the one it was created for, without a separate
commercial licence.

### 7.3 Commercial and extended licensing

Broader rights — including exclusive use, resale rights, extended
territory, extended duration, or use by a business other than the
commissioning client — are available only under a separate, specifically
priced commercial licence agreed in writing before or after delivery.
Contact ordift.ghana@gmail.com to discuss commercial licensing.

### 7.4 Unauthorised editing, resale, and misuse

Clients agree not to: significantly re-edit, alter, or apply filters to
delivered work in a way that misrepresents Ordift Studios's creative
work, without first removing any Ordift Studios credit/watermark from
the altered version; resell, license, or redistribute delivered work to
a third party as though it were the third party's own commissioned work;
or claim authorship or copyright of the work. Minor personal edits
(cropping for a specific platform, colour adjustment for personal use)
are permitted. A breach of this section is treated as a material breach
of the [Terms & Conditions](#2-terms--conditions) and may result in the
licence in §7.2 being revoked for the offending use.

### 7.5 Social media and credit

Ordift Studios may share final delivered work on its own portfolio,
website, and social media for promotional purposes, unless the client
requests otherwise in writing before or at the time of booking (for
example, for privacy-sensitive projects). Where Ordift Studios shares a
client's work, appropriate credit/tagging is given where reasonably
possible. Clients using delivered work on their own social media are
encouraged, but not required, to credit **@ordiftstudios** `[confirm
handle]`.

### 7.6 Third-party printing

Because print quality (colour calibration, paper stock, cropping)
materially affects how finished photography and design work is
represented, clients who wish to print delivered work through a printer
other than Ordift Studios's recommended print partner do so at their own
risk as to colour and quality accuracy. Ordift Studios is not
responsible for the print quality of any third-party printing, and print
files are provided "as delivered." Where a specific print licence or
print-ready file was not part of the agreed scope, additional fees may
apply to prepare files suitable for third-party printing.

### 7.7 Client-supplied materials

Where a client supplies logos, images, copy, or other materials for use
in a design, branding, or content project, the client warrants that they
own or are licensed to use those materials and indemnifies Ordift
Studios against any third-party claim arising from their use, consistent
with [Terms & Conditions §2.13](#2-terms--conditions).

### 7.8 Talent, model, and third-party rights

Where a project involves a model, instructor, vendor, or other talent
engaged by or through Ordift Studios, that individual's own image/
performance rights are governed by their separate engagement or release
agreement with Ordift Studios, copies of which are not shared with
clients but whose existence protects both the client's and the talent's
use of the resulting work within the scope agreed.

## 8. Client Deliverables Policy

**Last updated: `[date of approval]`**

This Policy explains what clients receive, when, and in what form, and
should be read with our [Copyright & Intellectual Property
Policy](#7-copyright--intellectual-property-policy).

### 8.1 What's included by default

Unless the signed quote says otherwise, a delivered project includes:
professionally selected and edited final images/footage/design files, in
standard web- and print-ready formats `[e.g. JPEG + print-resolution
TIFF/PDF as applicable]`; delivery via the Ordift Studios client portal
Deliverables gallery, as a link, or as staff determine most appropriate
for the file type and size; and one round of reasonable revisions to
the delivered edit, requested within `[e.g. 7 days]` of delivery and
consistent with the originally agreed brief/concept.

### 8.2 Turnaround time

Standard turnaround from the session/final asset handoff is:
photography — `[14–21 business days]`; videography — `[21–30 business
days]`, depending on project complexity; design/branding — per the
project timeline agreed at booking. Turnaround begins once all
information needed from the client (selects, brand assets, sign-off at
each stage) has been received — delays on the client's side extend the
turnaround accordingly, consistent with [Terms & Conditions
§2.5](#2-terms--conditions).

### 8.3 RAW and unedited files

**RAW/unedited source files are not included in standard delivery.**
Ordift Studios's editing and selection process is part of the creative
service being paid for, not a separable add-on that happens to be
withheld. RAW files may be made available only: as a specifically priced
add-on agreed **before** the session, or by separate written agreement
after the project, at Ordift Studios's discretion, and typically at a
premium reflecting the loss of exclusivity in the final edit. Requests
for RAW files made after project completion, without a prior agreement
covering them, may be declined or offered only at an additional fee set
by Ordift Studios.

### 8.4 Additional revisions and out-of-scope requests

Revision requests beyond §8.1's included round, or requests that go
beyond the originally agreed brief (new edits, additional images
selected, a different creative direction), are billed at Ordift
Studios's standard rate, quoted before work begins.

### 8.5 Delivery, retention, and archiving

Deliverables remain available in the client portal for `[90 days]` from
the date of publication. After that period, files are archived; a
client may request retrieval of archived files, which may carry a
reasonable retrieval fee reflecting storage and staff time. Ordift
Studios recommends clients download and back up their own copies of all
deliverables promptly after delivery — **Ordift Studios is not
responsible for loss of files after the retention period**, or for loss
caused by circumstances outside its control (storage-provider outage,
accidental client deletion).

### 8.6 Unrealistic delivery expectations

Ordift Studios will always confirm, at booking, a realistic turnaround
and scope for the specific project. Requests to compress an agreed
turnaround, add scope without a corresponding fee/timeline adjustment,
or receive same-day/rush delivery outside a specifically agreed and
paid rush service, may be declined or quoted separately as a rush fee.

### 8.7 Certificates (workshops)

Workshop completion certificates, where offered, are issued per the
[Workshop Terms](#9-workshop-terms) and are not part of this Policy's
photography/videography/design deliverable terms.

## 9. Workshop Terms

**Last updated: `[date of approval]`**

masterclass, or training session ("**Workshop**") and apply alongside

### 9.1 Registration

Registering for a Workshop reserves a place subject to availability and
payment as set out at registration. Where a Workshop is full, a
registrant may be placed on a waiting list and will be notified in the
event a place opens up.

### 9.2 Payment

Full payment `[or a specified deposit — confirm]` is due at registration
unless the Workshop listing states otherwise. A place is not guaranteed
until payment is received.

### 9.3 Attendance policy

Registrants are expected to arrive on time and attend for the full
published duration. Ordift Studios reserves the right to: deny late
entry more than `[e.g. 15 minutes]` after the published start time,
where doing so would disrupt the group; and decline to issue a
completion certificate to a registrant who misses more than
`[e.g. 20%]` of the published session time, at instructor discretion.
Equipment, materials, and venue rules communicated by the instructor at
the start of a Workshop must be followed; Ordift Studios may remove a
disruptive participant without refund.

### 9.4 Cancellation by the registrant

| Notice before Workshop | Outcome |
|---|---|
| More than `[7]` days | Full refund, or transfer to a future Workshop |
| `[3–7]` days | 50% refund, or full-value transfer to a future Workshop |
| Less than `[3]` days, or no-show | No refund; transfer at Ordift Studios's discretion |

If Ordift Studios cancels or postpones a Workshop (including for low

### 9.6 Materials and recordings

Workshop materials provided to participants are for personal learning

[Copyright & Intellectual Property Policy](#7-copyright--intellectual-property-policy).
Where a Workshop is recorded, participants will be informed in advance
and any use of participant likeness in promotional material follows
§7.5's social media terms, with the added protection that Workshop
recordings are not published without reasonable notice to registered
participants.

### 9.7 Certificates

Where a Workshop offers a completion certificate, it is issued after the
attendance requirements in §9.3 are met, via the client portal.
