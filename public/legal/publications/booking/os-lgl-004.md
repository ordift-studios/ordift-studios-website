## Part A — Introduction

<a id="purpose"></a>
### 1. Purpose

These [Booking](#definitions-booking) Terms & Conditions (“Agreement”) govern every booking, quotation, reservation, commission, consultation, workshop, production, and creative engagement entered into with [Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios).

This Agreement establishes the rights, responsibilities, and expectations of both [Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios) and its clients, ensuring that projects are delivered professionally, transparently, and fairly.

These Terms apply unless a separate written agreement expressly overrides specific provisions.

<a id="definitions"></a>
### 2. Definitions

For the purposes of this Agreement, the terms below have the meanings set out in the Definitions panel accompanying this document.

Terms defined below are specific to this document. Ordift Studios maintains a Master Definitions Register (OS-LGL-000) across the Enterprise Legal Series; where a term used in this Agreement is not defined below, its meaning in the Master Definitions Register applies.

<a id="definitions-ordift-studios"></a>**“Ordift Studios”** (also “we”, “our”, or “us”) refers to Ordift Studios, its owners, employees, authorised representatives, contractors, subcontractors, and approved creative partners acting within the scope of an engagement.

<a id="definitions-client"></a>**“Client”** refers to any individual, business, organisation, institution, or authorised representative requesting, booking, or receiving services from Ordift Studios.

<a id="definitions-booking"></a>**“Booking”** means any confirmed reservation for services, whether arranged through the website, email, telephone, WhatsApp, social media, the Client Portal, or a signed agreement.

<a id="definitions-services"></a>**“Services”** includes all current and future offerings of Ordift Studios, including photography, videography, creative direction, commercial productions, editing, workshops, consultancy, talent management, digital products, and any related professional services.

<a id="definitions-creative-works"></a>**“Creative Works”** means all photographs, videos, audio recordings, graphics, designs, documents, marketing materials, concepts, and other original works produced by or on behalf of Ordift Studios.

<a id="definitions-deliverables"></a>**“Deliverables”** means the final materials agreed to be provided to the Client under the relevant booking.

<a id="definitions-business-day"></a>**“Business Day”** means any day on which Ordift Studios is ordinarily open for business, excluding officially observed public holidays in the applicable operating jurisdiction unless otherwise stated.

<a id="scope-of-application"></a>
### 3. Scope of Application

These Terms apply to every service offered by [Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios), including future services unless a separate service-specific agreement expressly replaces or supplements these Terms.

Where a project includes additional documentation—such as a proposal, quotation, statement of work, event schedule, or licensing agreement—those documents form part of the overall agreement and should be read together.

If there is any inconsistency, the order of precedence shall be:

1. Signed Service Agreement or Contract
2. Approved Quotation or Proposal
3. Project-Specific Addendum
4. Licensing Agreement (where applicable)
5. These [Booking](#definitions-booking) Terms & Conditions
6. Website Terms of Use
7. Privacy Policy
8. Cookie Policy

<a id="acceptance-of-terms-part-a"></a>
### 4. Acceptance of Terms

A booking is considered accepted when any one of the following occurs:

- the [Client](#definitions-client) signs a service agreement;
- the [Client](#definitions-client) accepts or approves a quotation in writing;
- a required booking fee, deposit, retainer, or invoice is paid;
- the [Client](#definitions-client) confirms acceptance electronically (including via email or the [Client](#definitions-client) Portal);
- the [Client](#definitions-client) commences receipt of the [Services](#definitions-services);
- the [Client](#definitions-client) otherwise indicates acceptance; or
- [Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios) otherwise confirms the booking in writing.

By accepting a booking, the [Client](#definitions-client) confirms that they have read, understood, and agreed to be bound by these [Booking](#definitions-booking) Terms & Conditions together with all incorporated policies.

<a id="language-of-the-agreement"></a>
### 5. Language of the Agreement

The English version of this Agreement is the governing version. Where translations are provided for convenience, the English text prevails in the event of any inconsistency.

<a id="electronic-execution"></a>
### 6. Electronic Execution

Electronic signatures, electronic acceptances, payments, and confirmations made through the website, [Client](#definitions-client) Portal, or email are valid and have the same legal effect as handwritten signatures, where permitted by applicable law.

## Part B — Booking Process

<a id="booking-requests"></a>
### 7. Booking Requests

[Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios) welcomes booking enquiries from individuals, businesses, organisations, educational institutions, government agencies, non-profit organisations, and other lawful entities seeking our creative services.

A booking request may be submitted through:

- The [Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios) website;
- The [Client](#definitions-client) Portal;
- Email;
- Telephone;
- WhatsApp;
- Official social media accounts;
- Referral partners;
- In-person consultations; or
- Any other communication channel officially recognised by [Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios).

Submitting a booking request does not guarantee availability or create a contractual relationship.

Each request is subject to review based on factors including:

- Availability;
- Project scope;
- Required expertise;
- Scheduling feasibility;
- Resource availability;
- Compliance with applicable laws; and
- Alignment with [Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios)' professional standards and values.

[Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios) reserves the right to decline any booking request where, in its reasonable judgment:

- the requested services fall outside our expertise;
- the project presents legal, ethical, or safety concerns;
- the client provides misleading or incomplete information;
- the requested timeline is not reasonably achievable;
- the engagement could adversely affect the reputation, integrity, or operations of [Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios); or
- accepting the booking would otherwise be inappropriate.

Where a booking request is declined, [Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios) is under no obligation to provide detailed reasons, though we may do so at our discretion.

<a id="quotations-and-estimates"></a>
### 8. Quotations & Estimates

All quotations issued by [Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios) are prepared based on the information available at the time of issue.

Unless expressly stated otherwise:

- quotations are estimates for the requested scope of work;
- prices are quoted in United States Dollars (USD) as [Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios)' standard pricing currency;
- local currency conversions displayed on the website or quotations are provided for convenience and may vary due to exchange rate fluctuations;
- quotations are valid for 30 calendar days from the date of issue unless a different validity period is specified;
- quotations may be revised if the scope of work changes before acceptance.

A quotation does not reserve a date, time, team, equipment, or other resources unless confirmed in accordance with these Terms.

If the [Client](#definitions-client) requests changes to the scope, timeline, deliverables, or project requirements after a quotation has been issued, [Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios) reserves the right to revise the quotation accordingly.

Additional services requested after the commencement of a project may be charged separately.

<a id="booking-confirmation"></a>
### 9. Booking Confirmation

A booking is confirmed only when all required conditions specified by [Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios) have been satisfied.

These conditions may include, where applicable:

- acceptance of the quotation or proposal;
- execution of any required agreements;
- receipt of the required booking fee, retainer, or deposit;
- submission of requested project information;
- completion of onboarding requirements; and
- written confirmation issued by [Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios).

Until confirmation is issued, [Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios) may continue accepting other enquiries for the requested dates.

[Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios) is not responsible for losses arising from assumptions that a booking has been confirmed before the required confirmation process has been completed.

<a id="booking-fees-deposits-and-retainers"></a>
### 10. Booking Fees, Deposits & Retainers

To secure a booking, [Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios) may require a booking fee, deposit, or retainer, depending on the nature of the engagement.

For clarity:

- A [Booking](#definitions-booking) Fee reserves the agreed date and resources.
- A Deposit is an advance payment credited toward the total project cost.
- A Retainer compensates [Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios) for reserving availability and may be non-refundable except where expressly stated otherwise.

The applicable payment structure, including the specific amounts or percentages required, will be specified in the quotation, proposal, or service agreement rather than fixed in this Agreement, so that requirements can reasonably vary by project type.

Unless otherwise agreed in writing:

- no booking is secured until the required initial payment has been received and acknowledged;
- receipt of a deposit does not automatically transfer ownership of any [Creative Works](#definitions-creative-works);
- deposits and retainers may be applied only to the booking for which they were paid.

<a id="payment-terms"></a>
### 11. Payment Terms

The [Client](#definitions-client) agrees to pay all fees in accordance with the approved quotation, invoice, or written agreement.

Unless otherwise stated:

- invoices must be paid by the due date shown on the invoice;
- late payments may result in suspension of work or delivery;
- [Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios) may withhold deliverables until all outstanding amounts have been paid, where permitted by law and contract;
- recurring services must remain current to avoid interruption.

[Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios) reserves the right to charge reasonable late payment interest or recovery costs where permitted by applicable law. Any such charges will be disclosed in advance or specified in the relevant agreement.

<a id="currency-and-exchange-rates"></a>
### 12. Currency & Exchange Rates

[Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios) maintains United States Dollars (USD) as its primary pricing currency.

Where prices are displayed in Ghanaian Cedi (GHS), Qatari Riyal (QAR), or any other currency, those amounts are converted for convenience using exchange rate information available at the relevant time.

Because exchange rates fluctuate:

- converted prices may differ slightly from the final amount charged;
- payment processors or financial institutions may apply their own exchange rates or fees;
- [Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios) is not responsible for additional charges imposed by third-party payment providers.

The invoiced USD amount shall prevail unless otherwise agreed in writing.

### 13. Taxes, Duties & Government Charges

Unless expressly stated otherwise, quotations will indicate whether applicable taxes are included or excluded.

The [Client](#definitions-client) is responsible for any taxes, duties, permits, customs charges, withholding taxes, or government-imposed fees applicable to the project in the relevant jurisdiction, except where the law requires [Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios) to collect or remit such amounts.

For international assignments, the [Client](#definitions-client) is also responsible for assisting with any documentation reasonably required to facilitate lawful performance of the services, including permits or approvals where applicable.

## Part C — Scheduling & Logistics

This Part addresses the realities of creative work: changing schedules, weather, travel, venue restrictions, and unforeseen delays. It establishes clear expectations before any assigned personnel arrive on location.

<a id="scheduling"></a>
### 19. Scheduling

[Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios) will make reasonable efforts to provide the [Services](#definitions-services) on the agreed date(s), time(s), and location(s) confirmed in the booking.

Scheduling is based on the information supplied by the [Client](#definitions-client) at the time of booking. Any subsequent changes to dates, locations, timelines, or project requirements may require approval by [Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios) and may result in additional charges or revised delivery timelines.

The [Client](#definitions-client) is responsible for ensuring that all event details, addresses, schedules, and contact information provided to [Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios) are accurate and communicated in a timely manner.

[Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios) reserves the right to adjust internal staffing, equipment allocation, or production schedules where reasonably necessary to deliver the [Services](#definitions-services) effectively, provided such adjustments do not materially reduce the agreed scope of work.

<a id="rescheduling-part-c"></a>
### 20. Rescheduling

The procedure, approval factors, and financial treatment applicable to rescheduling a confirmed booking are set out in Part F (Cancellations, Rescheduling & Refunds).

<a id="travel"></a>
### 21. Travel

Where [Services](#definitions-services) require travel, the [Client](#definitions-client) shall provide accurate information regarding:

- venue location;
- access procedures;
- parking arrangements;
- accommodation (where applicable);
- local transportation requirements; and
- any known travel restrictions.

Travel fees, accommodation, visas, permits, tolls, parking, baggage charges, ferry costs, or other travel-related expenses shall be specified in the quotation or agreement where applicable.

[Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios) will make reasonable travel arrangements but is not responsible for delays caused by airlines, transportation providers, immigration authorities, customs, road closures, strikes, or similar circumstances beyond its reasonable control.

<a id="accommodation"></a>
### 22. Accommodation

For assignments requiring overnight travel or extended production periods, the [Client](#definitions-client) may be responsible for providing or reimbursing reasonable accommodation for the assigned team, as specified in the quotation or agreement.

Accommodation should be safe, hygienic, and reasonably located in relation to the project venue.

Where accommodation arranged by the [Client](#definitions-client) does not meet reasonable health or safety standards, [Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios) reserves the right to secure alternative accommodation, with the reasonable additional cost becoming payable by the [Client](#definitions-client).

<a id="permits-and-venue-access"></a>
### 23. Permits & Venue Access

The [Client](#definitions-client) is responsible for obtaining all permissions, licences, permits, or authorisations necessary for [Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios) to lawfully perform the [Services](#definitions-services) at the chosen location unless otherwise agreed in writing.

This includes, where applicable:

- venue permissions;
- photography permits;
- filming permits;
- drone permissions;
- government approvals;
- access credentials;
- security clearances; and
- permissions from property owners.

[Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios) is not responsible for delays or restrictions resulting from the [Client](#definitions-client)'s failure to obtain the necessary approvals.

<a id="delays"></a>
### 24. Delays

Creative productions frequently involve multiple participants and external factors.

If delays occur due to circumstances attributable to the [Client](#definitions-client) or third parties engaged by the [Client](#definitions-client), including but not limited to:

- late arrivals;
- incomplete preparation;
- venue delays;
- supplier delays;
- schedule overruns; or
- failure to provide agreed access,

[Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios) will make reasonable efforts to continue providing the [Services](#definitions-services) within operational limits.

Where delays substantially extend the agreed coverage period, additional time may be billed at the applicable hourly or daily extension rates specified in the quotation or agreement.

[Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios) is not obligated to extend coverage where doing so would conflict with another confirmed engagement or create unreasonable operational hardship.

<a id="weather"></a>
### 25. Weather

Some services, particularly outdoor photography, videography, and aerial productions, are dependent upon suitable weather conditions.

[Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios) will work collaboratively with the [Client](#definitions-client) to determine whether weather conditions make it appropriate to:

- proceed as planned;
- modify the production;
- relocate the session;
- temporarily pause work; or
- reschedule the affected portion of the project.

Safety shall always take precedence over production objectives.

[Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios) reserves the right to suspend or postpone activities where weather conditions present a genuine risk to personnel, equipment, participants, or the public.

Such decisions will be made reasonably and in good faith.

<a id="vendor-coordination"></a>
### 26. Vendor Coordination

While [Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios) will cooperate with planners, DJs, decorators, MCs, caterers, and other vendors, it cannot be held responsible for delays or disruptions caused by them.

<a id="exclusive-access"></a>
### 27. Exclusive Access

The [Client](#definitions-client) should ensure [Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios) has reasonable access to key moments, locations, and subjects necessary to perform the agreed [Services](#definitions-services). Persistent obstruction by guests or other vendors may affect the final deliverables.

<a id="client-representative"></a>
### 28. Client Representative

For larger events, the [Client](#definitions-client) shall designate one authorised representative who can make binding decisions on-site. This avoids conflicting instructions from multiple people during a production.

<a id="drone-and-restricted-airspace"></a>
### 29. Drone & Restricted Airspace

For projects involving drones, operations remain subject to applicable aviation regulations, weather conditions, safety assessments, and regulatory approvals. If drone flights cannot legally or safely proceed, [Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios) will not be considered in breach of the agreement.

<a id="meals-and-rest-breaks"></a>
### 30. Meals & Rest Breaks

For productions exceeding a specified duration (for example, 6–8 continuous hours), the [Client](#definitions-client) should provide reasonable meal breaks and refreshments for the production team, or alternatively reimburse agreed meal costs.

## Part D — Service Delivery

This Part governs how [Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios) performs its work, what clients should expect from the creative process, and how professional judgment is exercised.

<a id="creative-process"></a>
### 31. Creative Process

[Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios) approaches every project as a collaborative creative partnership.

While clients are encouraged to share ideas, inspiration, references, mood boards, objectives, and preferences, the methods, techniques, equipment selection, lighting approach, composition, editing workflow, and overall artistic execution remain the professional responsibility of [Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios).

Every project is unique. As such, no two projects will produce identical results, even where similar concepts, locations, or styles are requested.

[Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios) reserves the right to determine the most appropriate creative approach required to achieve the agreed objectives while maintaining [Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios)' artistic standards and professional reputation.

<a id="client-responsibilities-during-service-delivery"></a>
### 32. Client Responsibilities During Service Delivery

To facilitate successful delivery of the [Services](#definitions-services), the [Client](#definitions-client) agrees to:

- provide accurate and timely information;
- communicate any changes promptly;
- ensure all required permissions have been obtained;
- ensure participants arrive at agreed times;
- coordinate access to venues and locations;
- provide a safe working environment;
- appoint a decision-maker for larger productions where requested;
- notify [Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios) of any accessibility, cultural, religious, or special requirements that may affect the production.

The [Client](#definitions-client) acknowledges that delays, incomplete information, lack of preparation, or failure to meet these responsibilities may affect the final outcome.

<a id="artistic-discretion"></a>
### 33. Artistic Discretion

One of the principal reasons clients engage [Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios) is for its creative expertise and artistic judgment.

Accordingly, [Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios) retains sole discretion regarding:

- posing direction;
- framing and composition;
- camera placement;
- lighting;
- colour grading;
- retouching techniques;
- editing style;
- storytelling approach;
- music selection (for audiovisual productions, where applicable);
- sequencing of images or videos;
- album layouts;
- graphic presentation; and
- final creative decisions.

While reasonable client preferences will always be considered, [Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios) is not obligated to replicate another photographer's, videographer's, designer's, or studio's style exactly.

References supplied by clients are treated as inspiration rather than guaranteed replication.

<a id="equipment-and-technical-standards"></a>
### 34. Equipment & Technical Standards

[Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios) maintains professional equipment suitable for the agreed [Services](#definitions-services).

Equipment selection remains solely at the discretion of [Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios) and may include:

- cameras;
- lenses;
- lighting systems;
- audio equipment;
- drones (where permitted);
- stabilisation systems;
- computers;
- software;
- cloud infrastructure; and
- other professional production tools.

[Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios) reserves the right to substitute equivalent or superior equipment where operationally necessary.

Clients may not require the use of specific equipment unless expressly agreed in writing before the engagement.

<a id="backup-equipment-and-contingency-planning"></a>
### 35. Backup Equipment & Contingency Planning

[Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios) recognises the importance of reliability.

Where reasonably practicable, backup equipment, storage media, batteries, memory cards, and other essential resources will be maintained to minimise disruption arising from technical failures.

Despite reasonable precautions, unforeseen equipment failures may occur.

Where such failures materially affect the [Services](#definitions-services), [Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios) will make reasonable efforts to repair, replace, or otherwise mitigate the impact.

No creative service can guarantee absolute immunity from technical failures.

<a id="personnel-assistants-and-contractors"></a>
### 36. Personnel, Assistants & Contractors

[Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios) may engage employees, assistants, second photographers, second videographers, editors, retouchers, designers, drone operators, production assistants, or independent contractors to assist with delivering the [Services](#definitions-services).

All such personnel operate under the direction or quality standards established by [Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios).

The assignment of personnel remains at the discretion of [Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios) unless specific individuals have been expressly guaranteed within a written agreement.

<a id="editing-and-post-production"></a>
### 37. Editing & Post-Production

Editing forms an essential part of the creative service provided by [Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios).

Unless otherwise agreed:

- colour correction;
- exposure balancing;
- cropping;
- composition refinement;
- standard retouching;
- audio enhancement;
- colour grading;
- rendering;
- export optimisation; and
- quality control

are performed according to [Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios)' artistic style and technical standards.

Advanced retouching, object removal, body modifications, extensive compositing, CGI, AI-assisted enhancements, restoration, or additional revisions beyond the agreed scope may incur additional charges.

<a id="turnaround-times"></a>
### 38. Turnaround Times

Estimated delivery times provided by [Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios) are good-faith estimates based on the agreed project scope and current production schedule.

While every reasonable effort will be made to meet estimated delivery dates, they do not constitute guaranteed deadlines unless expressly agreed in writing.

Delivery timelines may reasonably be extended due to:

- unusually large projects;
- extensive revisions;
- client delays;
- force majeure events;
- technical failures;
- additional services requested after production; or
- other circumstances beyond [Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios)' reasonable control.

Clients will be informed where significant delays are anticipated.

<a id="preview-galleries-and-proofs"></a>
### 39. Preview Galleries & Proofs

Where preview galleries, contact sheets, or proofing systems are provided, they are intended solely for review and selection purposes.

Unless expressly authorised:

- previews may not be downloaded for commercial use;
- screenshots should not be publicly distributed;
- watermarks must not be removed;
- previews remain protected by copyright.

[Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios) may limit the duration for which preview galleries remain accessible.

<a id="final-delivery"></a>
### 40. Final Delivery

Final deliverables will be supplied in the formats specified within the quotation, proposal, or agreement.

In the event of a suspected or confirmed personal data breach, [Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios) is committed to investigating the matter, taking reasonable steps to contain and remediate the breach, and notifying affected individuals and/or the relevant regulatory authorities where required by applicable law.

This Appendix is a summary of [Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios)' commitment in principle. It does not describe specific internal procedures, which may be adopted and updated by [Ordift Studios](#definitions-ordift-studios) from time to time.
