Used as the fallback for Arabic script in the quotation PDF (src/lib/pdf/fonts.ts, BODY_FONT); Inter remains the Latin face.
Why not Noto Sans Arabic: tested first. It builds dotted Arabic letters from separate mark glyphs, and the PDF renderer (react-pdf) places those marks wrongly (letters such as final yeh and seen lost or displaced their dots). IBM Plex Sans Arabic uses complete letter shapes and renders correctly.
