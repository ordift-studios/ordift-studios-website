# Booking notices v2: reschedule / cancellation notices, verified delivery, release plan (10 Nov 2026) — LOCAL; nothing applied to Production

## 1. What is built
**Client notices (separate, each describing the CURRENT booking):** Booking Confirmed (as before); **Rescheduled and confirmed** ("Previously" = the date the client was last actually TOLD, "Now confirmed" = the current one; no invented "previously" if they were never told); **Cancelled — new date to be agreed**; **Cancelled (final)** (says it will not be rescheduled; mentions money only if the client paid, and only that nothing was changed or refunded by the cancellation). If a newer booking version exists when a notice is about to go, it is marked *superseded* and not sent, so an outdated date cannot go out (also on retry, and when staff record a delivery).
**Default is to tell the client.** A Super Admin may hold a CHANGE or CANCELLATION notice back only with a written reason (at least a sentence): recorded on the notice (who, when, why), written to the audit log, and shown as an **Outstanding action** on the enquiry until it is sent ("Send now") or staff record that they told the client another way. A first confirmation can never be held back. If the deferral cannot be recorded the notice simply goes out (the safe direction) and the person is told.
**Delivery tracking, idempotency, retry, failure:** the same outbox as before (one job per booking version and audience, atomic claim, sent rows immutable, staff-triggered Retry, every outcome audited).
**Cancellation outcomes:** the cancel form asks "a new date will be agreed" (project reads "Booking Cancelled — Rescheduling Pending", the default) or "Final" (reads "Booking Cancelled"). Both keep the accepted quotation, the original booking row (append-only), every payment and the audit trail; neither refunds, changes a balance or closes the project (staff decide those separately; the screen and the final notice say so). Stage goes back from Booked to Quotation Sent in both cases (existing status).

## 2. Staff-recorded enquiries and verified delivery
Rule (pure, tested): a notice with booking details goes only to an address the system can stand behind.
1. Project has a **portal owner** (the client claimed it with the verified link-and-code process, or signed up from a website enquiry): the **owner's confirmed account address**, never the address typed on the enquiry. Unconfirmed or unreadable account address: nothing is sent (no fall-back).
2. No owner, **website-form** enquiry: the typed address (existing policy for quotation and payment e-mails; flagged below).
3. No owner, **staff-recorded** (WhatsApp, telephone, e-mail, in person): **never auto-mailed**, whatever the enquiry's e-mail-suppression setting says. A lab record that matched an existing account's address was correctly NOT mailed. Shared mailboxes and wrong addresses are therefore never reached.
**Staff workflow when e-mail is unavailable:** the Notifications box shows the exact message for the current booking and an Outstanding action ("tell the client yourself"); staff tell the client (WhatsApp, telephone, e-mail from their own mailbox, in person) and press **Record that I told the client** with the channel and an optional note (the note may not contain a phone number or e-mail address); an Admin or Super Admin can do it; it is audited (`booking.client_notified_manually`), the record shows who and how, and it cannot also be e-mailed afterwards. The existing portal invitation card is the route to automatic notices for that client from then on.

## 3. Internal notices, by role
- **Assigned person** (new): told when assigned (confirmation), when their booking changes (Was / Now), when it is cancelled (new date may follow / cancelled for good, release the time), and a **person taken off** a booking is told so. Content is operational only: reference, service, client's first name, session in local time and UTC, place, notes. No money, agreement, quotation, client contact. Sent only if the person is active, has an internal role, has a confirmed login address, and has not switched the notice off (preference `assigned_booking`, default on; no screen to switch it off yet).
- **Team** (Super Admins and opted-in Admins, existing rule): Super Admins get the full notice; **Admins get the operational one with no payment condition, amounts or basis** (consistent with what the booking card shows them; the previous version would have e-mailed amounts to opted-in Admins).
- An **Admin** sees only the client notice records in the booking card (no internal or assigned notices, no Retry); a Super Admin sees all.

## 4. Release-safe plan to disable payment-triggered booking
Written in `RELEASE_PLAN_payment_auto_booking_2026_11_10.md` with a read-only diagnostic (`DIAGNOSTIC_payment_auto_booking_cutover.sql`, six blocks). Summary: code assessment of what depends on the old behaviour (deliverables publishable only at Booked/In Progress; Live Operations; workflow summary; nothing reads the old audit actions); the **communication gap is closed in three layers**: the payment receipt (unchanged outbox, to the payer's account address) now carries a plain "this does not by itself confirm a date; you will receive a Booking Confirmed message" paragraph whenever payments do not book the project (tested; absent in legacy mode, workshops, Crew Support, already-booked projects); the portal and staff pages show "Paid — scheduling pending"; and the date message is the booking confirmation e-mail (Plan B) or staff contact (Plan A). Plan B (switch booking confirmation on, after four migrations) is recommended; Plan A (`PAYMENT_AUTO_BOOKS_ENQUIRY=false` alone) is a stop-gap that leaves no automatic date e-mail and needs a daily staff routine. Sequence, cutover window, canary, legacy records, rollback and open points are in the plan. **No environment variable was changed and nothing was deployed.**

## 5. Migration dependencies and rollback
Order, one per run, each separately approved: `PROPOSAL_booking_confirmation.sql` → `PROPOSAL_booking_cancel_reverts_stage.sql` → `PROPOSAL_booking_notifications.sql` → **`PROPOSAL_booking_notices_v2.sql`** (new: cancellation outcome column + constraint; jobs gain audience / event / status values and four columns; the job-creating trigger, claim and cancel functions are replaced; `defer_booking_client_notice` added; refuses to run without v1). Reload the API schema cache after each. Rollbacks beside each; `ROLLBACK_booking_notices_v2.sql` restores the v1 functions and **drops the new columns and v2-only jobs (cancellation outcomes and deferral reasons are lost: export first)**; verified apply-then-rollback in a rolled-back transaction. App compatibility: with v2 absent a default cancellation works as before (the outcome argument is only sent for a final cancellation, which then says it needs the update), the derived status falls back when the outcome column is missing, and notice dispatch is a quiet no-op without the tables.

## 7. Outstanding issues and decisions
1. **Website-form enquiries** with no portal owner are still mailed on the typed address (existing policy). Booking details to a typed, unverified address is a softer version of the staff-recorded risk. Decision: keep, or also require the portal route / a verified address?
2. **Final cancellation and the stage:** the project stays at Quotation Sent (not closed) so a paid-but-cancelled project is not hidden; decide who closes it and who decides refunds (a refund workflow is not built, by instruction).
3. **Assigned-person preference** (`assigned_booking`) has no screen yet; an explicit off row is honoured.
4. Retry is manual and the derived "Paid — scheduling pending" projects have no list filter yet (offered in the release plan).
5. Plan A vs Plan B for Production (release plan section 7) and what a manual move to Booked should send.
6. The lab keys expire every 30 days now; re-run the minting if a lab session shows empty Admin views again (it is a lab-only task).
