## How v1.1 is built
v1.0 is the existing Master Booking Terms (Parts A-K, clauses 1-163, source `src/lib/legal/documents/os-lgl-004-booking.ts`). v1.1 is **v1.0 plus a defined set of changes**, so that counsel reviews only what is new or different:
- **Parts A-K of v1.0 continue to apply word for word, except the nine amendments in section A below.**
- **A new Part L (Client Services Engagement Terms), section B below**, applies to an engagement formed from an accepted ordinary quotation.
- **Schedule 1 (Engagement Particulars), section C**, is the one-page record of the facts of the engagement. It is filled from recorded facts by the system and never retyped.
Bookings already confirmed under v1.0 stay under v1.0 (v1.0 clause 140).

## B. NEW PART L - CLIENT SERVICES ENGAGEMENT TERMS (proposed wording)

**L1. Engagement Particulars.** Schedule 1 forms part of this Agreement. If Schedule 1 and the Accepted Quotation differ, Schedule 1 prevails for the facts it records; the Accepted Quotation prevails for price, currency and scope.

**L2. Contracting Entity and licensed activities.** The Contracting Entity named in Schedule 1 provides the Services. It performs only the activities it is licensed or registered to perform in its jurisdiction, as recorded when the Accepted Quotation was issued. Nothing in this Agreement extends any licence or registration, and an agreement cannot make an unlicensed activity permitted. **[COUNSEL]**

**L3. Price and currency.** The price is the total stated in the Accepted Quotation in its currency, as repeated in Schedule 1. Clause 12 (as amended) applies.

**L4. Payment before confirmation.** Schedule 1 repeats the payment condition of the Accepted Quotation word for word. Where it requires a deposit or full payment, the Contracting Entity is not obliged to reserve the date or resources until the payment has been verified.

**L5. Schedule and place.** Schedule 1 records each session: date, start and end, time zone and place (on site, studio or online with the platform). A multi-day or multi-location engagement has one entry per session. A change to the schedule takes effect only when recorded by Ordift Studios as a change to the booking and, where the engagement needs an agreement, remains subject to this Agreement. **[COUNSEL: weather and force-majeure rescheduling for on-site sessions; the Client's duty to give access.]**

**L6. Minors and vulnerable persons.** Where the Services involve a person under the legal age of majority or a vulnerable adult, the Client confirms before the session that a parent, guardian or other person with authority has consented in the form Ordift Studios provides, and Ordift Studios may apply safeguarding conditions and decline or stop work that does not meet them. **[COUNSEL: the age and consent rules in Qatar and in Ghana; storage, use and portfolio use of images of children; whether portfolio use (clause 54) must be excluded for minors unless separately consented.]**

**L7. Restricted or sensitive locations and permits.** Clause 23 applies. Where Schedule 1 records a restricted or sensitive location, the Client must provide the permit or permission in writing before the session and Ordift Studios may postpone or refuse the session until it is provided. **[COUNSEL: drone and airspace rules; filming permits in each jurisdiction.]**

**L8. Extended usage and exclusivity.** Standard usage is as set out in Part E. Any usage beyond it (territory, duration, media, advertising, resale) and any exclusivity are granted only if stated in Schedule 1 with their price. Clause 54 (Ordift's portfolio and promotional rights) continues to apply unless Schedule 1 records a written promotional restriction. **[COUNSEL: whether clause 54's perpetual right is acceptable to individual and corporate clients; the interaction with the recorded-permission process for portfolio use.]**

**L9. Third-party material, releases and clearances.** Schedule 1 records which party obtains releases from identifiable people, music clearances and location permission. Absent an entry, the Client does. Clause 57 continues to apply. **[COUNSEL]**

**L10. Changes after signature.** A change to scope, price, schedule or usage is made only in writing, is recorded in a new version of Schedule 1 or an addendum, and is checked against Ordift Studios' booking rules (which may require a new agreement or a new confirmation). Clause 140 applies.

**L11. Execution.** The parties sign through the platform described in A9. The Contracting Entity signs through its authorised signatory recorded in Schedule 1, with the authority recorded there. The Client signs personally (an individual) or through the authorised representative named in Schedule 1 (an organisation). **[COUNSEL: signatory capacity wording for each entity.]**
