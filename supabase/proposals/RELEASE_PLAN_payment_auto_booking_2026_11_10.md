# Release-safe plan: stop a payment from booking a project (10 Nov 2026) — PLAN ONLY; nothing in Production is changed, no environment variable, no deployment

## 1. What the old behaviour does and what depends on it (code assessment; Production data itself was not read)
- **Behaviour (Production today):** when an ordinary enquiry's completed payments reach its amount due, `advanceStageOnFullPayment` moves it to "booked", e-mails the client a generic "Booking Confirmed" (no date, no place) and the team a "New booking" notice. No booking record exists in Production (the booking tables are not there).
- **What depends on the stage "booked":** (1) publishing deliverables is allowed only at booked or in-progress (`deliverables/actions.ts`); (2) Live Operations lists booked-onward projects (`liveOperations.ts`); (3) the enquiry workflow summary shows "booking done" from booked onward; (4) Crew Support is already independent (booked only by its own confirmation). Nothing reads the two audit actions `enquiry.booking_confirmed_email_sent` / `enquiry.new_booking_notification_sent` except the function that writes them. Workshops are not affected.
- **Consequence of disabling it:** a paid project stays at Quotation Sent until a person marks it Booked (existing stage control, available while booking confirmation is off) or confirms a booking (when it is on). Until then it cannot receive deliverables and does not appear in Live Operations: correct, but staff must act.

## 2. The communication gap, and how it is closed
Without the old behaviour a client who pays could be left with a receipt that says "Paid" and nothing about their date. Three layers close it:
1. **Payment acknowledgement is independent and unchanged:** the receipt e-mail (durable outbox, retry) keeps going to the payer's account address. New in this release: while payments do not book the project, the receipt carries a plain "what happens next" paragraph ("your payment has been received; it does not by itself confirm a date; you will receive a Booking Confirmed message with the date, time and place"). Tested; absent in legacy mode, for workshops, Crew Support and projects already booked.
2. **Clear status in the portal and for staff:** "Paid — scheduling pending" / "Payment received — scheduling pending" on the client's dashboard, header and card, and an amber box on the staff enquiry page.
3. **A real scheduling message when the date is set:** the Booking Confirmed e-mail with date, time, zone and place, sent by a booking confirmation (needs the booking tables). Without them (opt-out only) staff tell the client themselves.

## 3. Two ways to go, and the recommendation
- **Plan B (recommended, the target): switch on booking confirmation.** A payment then never books (the same gate), and everything above exists: confirmation e-mail, reschedule and cancellation notices, assigned-person notices, outstanding-action tracking. Needs the four SQL proposals applied first, in this order, one per run: `PROPOSAL_booking_confirmation.sql`, `PROPOSAL_booking_cancel_reverts_stage.sql`, `PROPOSAL_booking_notifications.sql`, `PROPOSAL_booking_notices_v2.sql` (plus `PROPOSAL_crew_support_booking_overlap_guard.sql` if Crew Support requests are live). Reload the API schema cache after each.
- **Plan A (stop-gap only): `PAYMENT_AUTO_BOOKS_ENQUIRY=false` with the switch still off.** No migrations. Payment is recorded and acknowledged (receipt with the new paragraph), the portal and staff show "scheduling pending", no generic "Booking Confirmed" is sent. **The gap that remains:** no automatic date e-mail at all. Use only with a written staff routine: each working day review projects reading "Paid — scheduling pending", contact the client, then set the stage to Booked. No list view exists for that label yet (the diagnostic file's block 2 and the enquiry page are the tools); say if you want a filter on /admin/enquiries as a small follow-up.

## 4. Sequence (every step needs your separate written approval; none is requested now)
0. **Read-only look first:** a person runs `DIAGNOSTIC_payment_auto_booking_cutover.sql` and `DIAGNOSTIC_booked_without_booking_record.sql` on Production. They show: legacy projects booked by payment, paid-but-unbooked projects, payments that could land during the change, how many generic Booking Confirmed e-mails have gone, and any completed payment whose receipt did not send (an acknowledgement gap that exists TODAY).
1. **Release the application code** (inert by default: with neither switch set, behaviour is exactly today's; the receipt paragraph does not appear). Verify one receipt and one booking e-mail are unchanged.
2. **Plan B only: apply the four migrations**, one per run, each with its rehearsal and rollback ready; reload the schema cache; check the tables and functions exist (read-only). The card stays hidden while the switch is off, so verify with the read-only diagnostics instead.
3. **Choose the moment:** a quiet hour, with no payment in `awaiting_verification` that a person is about to approve (diagnostic block 4). Payments completing within seconds of the change are decided by whichever code is running at that instant (the stage update is one atomic conditional statement, so nothing is double-applied); anything booked by the old code in the last minutes appears in diagnostic block 1 afterwards and is handled as a legacy project.
4. **Flip:** Plan B: set `BOOKING_CONFIRMATION_ENABLED=true` (this alone stops payments booking). Plan A: set `PAYMENT_AUTO_BOOKS_ENQUIRY=false`. Redeploy as the platform requires. (Both are environment settings: reversible within minutes, no data change.)
5. **Scoped canary** (separately authorised, per the standing no-staging rule): one internal test enquiry through payment → receipt with the new paragraph → portal label → (Plan B) booking confirmation e-mail. Check, then stop.
6. **Watch for a week:** receipt jobs (diagnostic block 6), the Notifications box (Plan B), "Paid — scheduling pending" projects older than your service level.

## 5. Existing Production records
- **Legacy "booked by payment" projects stay booked** (nothing is rewritten). With Plan B they read "Booked (no booking record)" to staff only (the client sees "Scheduling pending" only if you later cancel; otherwise "Booked"). Do not retro-confirm them blindly: a confirmation sends the client a Booking Confirmed message, and they already received the generic one. Where a date is known and a proper record is wanted, confirm it deliberately and accept the e-mail, or leave as legacy.
- **Delivery eligibility:** projects at Booked or In Progress can receive deliverables; a paid project awaiting scheduling cannot until it is booked.
- **Existing paid-in-full, not booked projects** (diagnostic block 2) simply show the new status.

## 6. Rollback
Plan A: unset the variable (minutes). Plan B: set the switch off again (the booking card disappears; payments book again as before), and, only if needed, run the rollback files in reverse order (`ROLLBACK_booking_notices_v2.sql` drops outcomes and deferral records, `ROLLBACK_booking_notifications.sql` drops the delivery records: export first). Code: revert the release (the gates are early returns). Nothing in this plan edits an existing payment, quotation or booking row.

## 7. Open points for your decision
1. Plan A as an interim, or wait for Plan B. 2. Whether a manual move to Booked (switch off) should send the client a Booking Confirmed message (today it sends nothing). 3. Whether you want a /admin/enquiries filter for "Paid — scheduling pending". 4. Who owns the daily review under Plan A.
