# Payment integrity review (9 Nov 2026) — LOCAL; read-only except on synthetic data in the disposable lab; nothing applied to Production

Scope: card payment starts, provider callbacks, payment confirmation, bank-transfer start, proof submission and bank-transfer reconciliation. Method: code reading (`checkoutService.ts`, `gatewaySync.ts`, the Paystack webhook route, `reconcilePendingPayment.ts`, the bank-transfer route, `bankTransferVerification.ts`, migrations 0024, 0028, 0029, 0030, 0151), plus tests on a disposable copy of the lab database. Per your instruction, nothing below beyond the bank-transfer start safeguard has been implemented.

## 1. Attempt versus confirmed payment
- A payment ATTEMPT is a `payments` row in status `pending` (card started, or bank-transfer instruction awaiting the transfer), `awaiting_verification` (proof uploaded), `additional_info_required`, `failed` or `rejected`. A CONFIRMED payment is a row in status `completed`.
- Collected amounts are never summed from attempts. Both writers of `enquiries.amount_paid` / `workshop_registrations.amount_paid` (`syncEntityPaymentStatus` in `gatewaySync.ts`, and the bank-transfer sync in `admin/payments/actions.ts`) recompute it from `status = 'completed'` rows only (refund rows subtract). Workshop financial overview reads `amount_paid`. The verification function also bounds a credit by completed payments only.
- A card payment becomes `completed` only after the provider reports success AND the reported amount and currency match the locked amount (otherwise it is marked `failed` with a `payment.amount_mismatch` entry). A bank transfer becomes `completed` only through `verify_bank_transfer_payment`, which requires the verifier to confirm what actually arrived; a receipt upload never completes anything.
- Verified in the lab on the real running app: a refused card start left one `failed` row and `amount_paid` unchanged (0 of 1200).

## 2. What is already well protected
- Card start: partial unique index (migration 0028, one pending card payment per record and type), reconciliation against the provider before a second attempt, a 5 minute grace period, a rate limit.
- Provider callbacks: signature (HMAC-SHA512) checked on the raw body, replay window, an idempotency key stored in a table, every event logged, amount and currency compared.
- Completion: an atomic claim table (0029/0030), step-granular markers, the activity entry and the receipt job created atomically, a durable receipt outbox. Refunds have their own idempotency key.
- Bank reconciliation: one row-locked database function; one approval per payment; one approved credit per bank reference per receiving account; separation of duties (the payer or submitter cannot verify); currency mismatch blocked; excess must be acknowledged and is not credited; a credit cannot exceed what the receivable still owes (see finding 1 for a hole in that last check).

## 3. The bank-transfer start (item 2, implemented locally)
Gap: select-then-insert let two simultaneous starts both insert (reproduced: 6 open instructions from 6 simultaneous requests on a disposable copy). Safeguard: a partial unique index, one OPEN (`pending`) bank-transfer instruction per client, record and payment type, plus the route answering with the winner when the database catches the race. Files: `PROPOSAL_/ROLLBACK_/DIAGNOSTIC_bank_transfer_*`, `rehearse_bank_transfer_open_instruction.sh`, `rehearse_bank_transfer_concurrency.py`, and the route change with tests. Legitimate repeats (sequential instalments, retries after rejection or an information request, separate payment types, records and clients, unlimited history) are unaffected; rehearsed.

## 4. Additional issues found (reported, NOT implemented, awaiting your decision)

## 5. Lab observations (not product defects)
- The lab was missing the grant that lets a signed-in client read `current_exchange_rates` (migration 0024 grants it). Restored in the lab only; the checkout showed "Card and Mobile Money aren't available in GHS yet" until then.
- The lab has no payment provider configured and no provider key, so a card start can only end in "We couldn't start your payment" and a `failed` attempt. No request to a payment provider is possible from the lab.
- The rate limiter is in-process memory in the lab: 5 starts per 10 minutes per client and route.
